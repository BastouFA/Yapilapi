# Creator economy

Scope: `apps/api/src/modules/creator`, migrations `230_creator_economy.sql`, `231_subscribers_visibility.sql`, `232_creator_affiliate_optin.sql`, `scripts/creator-maintenance.ts` (`npm run creator:maintenance`).
Tests: `modules/creator/rules.unit.test.ts` (12, pure rules), `apps/api/test/creator.test.ts` (31, real Postgres through the HTTP API).
Everything is behind the `COMMERCE` flag (like the money it moves). Creators are adults: accounts under 18 cannot join, subscribe, tip or gift.

## Design

**One charging path.** Subscriptions, renewals, tips, gifts and partnership payments all call `chargeToCreator` (`charge.ts`): payment intent through the provider abstraction, idempotent by `(payer_id, idempotency_key)` plus a request hash. The provider's **signed webhook decides capture**; the creator module then pulls the result (`settleCreatorPayments`, `settleSimple`, `settlePartnershipPayments`) and applies effects exactly once (`subscription_payments.applied_at`). Money is booked in the existing double-entry ledger (`postLedger`): seller payable for the creator, `platform:fees` for the platform fee (`PLATFORM_FEE_BPS`). Payment methods are opaque provider tokens; a stored renewal method is AES-GCM encrypted (aad `sub-pm:<id>`) and wiped on cancel or account deletion. No card data, tokens or secrets reach logs or the audit trail.

**Subscriptions and dunning.** `incomplete -> active -> past_due -> expired|cancelled`. A failed renewal retries on day 1, 3 and 5 after the _first_ failure, then expires. Entitlement is `active` (+1 day slack for a late job) or `past_due` (+3 days grace). The same rule exists twice on purpose: SQL in `lib/visibility.ts` (feeds, search, profiles) and TypeScript in `creator/entitlements.ts` (`hasActiveSubscription`, used by live gating); both are covered by tests, including the boundary times. Renewals that get stuck (payment `requires_action` for 10 min, `authorized` for 24 h) are recovered by the maintenance job.

**Subscribers-only content.** `VISIBILITIES` gained `subscribers` (`packages/shared`). `postVisibleSql` has a `WHEN 'subscribers'` branch: viewer holds an entitled subscription to the author with plan tier >= `posts.metadata.minTier` (default 1). Because every feed, search, profile and AI tool already goes through `postVisibleSql`, the rule holds everywhere; the test asserts direct read, author page, feed and search. `createPost` refuses `subscribers` unless the author is an active creator.

**Affiliate.** Sellers opt products in with a commission cap; a creator link stores a commission within it. Clicks are deduplicated per visitor per day (HMAC visitor hash, no raw IP), bot user agents are recorded but never attributed. Conversions attach to paid orders inside an attribution window; commission is settled from the seller's payable to the creator only when the seller balance covers it, and reversed while unsettled if the order is refunded.

**Analytics privacy.** The dashboard reports aggregates only; the audience-by-country table applies k-anonymity (`AUDIENCE_K = 20`, smaller groups fold into `other`).

**Privacy hooks.** Export section `creator_economy` (never includes payment tokens). Deletion hook: cancels subscriptions and wipes stored payment methods, closes the creator account, deactivates plans and links. Ledger and payment history stay (financial records).

## Honest gaps

- **No clawback after affiliate settlement.** A refund after the commission was settled leaves the commission with the creator; only unsettled commissions are reversed.
- **Real KYC and payouts depend on the payment provider.** With the dev provider the KYC decision is a staff decision on submitted details; identity documents are never stored by us.
- **No tax handling** (VAT, 1099, withholding) and no multi-currency conversion: amounts are in the currency of the plan/gift.
- **Refunds of subscription periods and tips are manual** (staff via the payments module); there is no self-service pro-rata refund.
- Renewal charging is pull-based (a scheduled job); nothing in the API process schedules it. Run `npm run creator:maintenance` (or the admin endpoint) regularly.
- Gift catalog art and animations are out of scope; gifts are priced catalog entries.
