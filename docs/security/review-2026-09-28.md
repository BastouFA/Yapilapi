# Security review, 2026-09-28

A review of the features added on 2026-09-27 and 28, with the fixes made and the tests that keep
them fixed (`apps/api/test/security-review.test.ts`, the blocks after "Review of the features added
on 2026-09-27 and 28"). Each test was run against the code before its fix and failed there.

## Scope

Live location (`modules/location.ts`, `lib/location.ts`), Market (`modules/market.ts`,
`lib/market.ts`), event tickets and check-in (`modules/tickets.ts`, `lib/tickets.ts`), Echo
(`modules/echoes.ts`, `lib/echoes.ts`, the ffmpeg render), collages (`modules/collages.ts`,
`lib/collage.ts`), Together (`modules/together.ts`, `lib/together.ts`), Mixes, cover editing
(`lib/covers.ts`, `lib/cover-render.ts`), chat games with chess and the limits trigger (migration
0060), Ask me, drops, the store-purchase policy, the data export (`lib/data-export.ts`) and account
deletion. Every route in those modules was read for who may call it and what it returns.

Checked for: object-level authorization on every route, hidden data in responses, blocks and the
rules for people under 18, rate limits, SQL built from input, paths and ffmpeg/sharp arguments built
from input, URL fetching, unbounded work, races on stock, tickets and offers, ticket token forgery
and replay, CSRF, extra body fields, and who receives each realtime event.

## Findings

### 1. High: API keys and app tokens bypass their blocked routes with encoded paths

- **What:** keys and OAuth access tokens must never reach account security, the export, keys,
  payouts, consents, AI memory or `DELETE /v1/me`. The check compared regular expressions with
  `req.url` as sent, but the router decodes the path before matching. `GET /v1/me/%65xport` ran the
  data export, `GET /v1/%61uth/sessions` listed sign-in sessions and `DELETE /v1/%6De` deleted the
  account, all with a key or with an access token a third-party app got through "Sign in with
  YAPILAPI".
- **Where:** `apps/api/src/plugins/auth.ts` (`registerAuth`).
- **Fix:** the check now runs on the matched route's pattern (`req.routeOptions.url`) and on the
  decoded path, as well as the path as sent.
- **Test:** "developer keys and app tokens: refuses the export, sign-in sessions and account deletion
  however the path is spelled".

### 2. Medium: Together albums and the rule for people under 18

- **What:** everyone in an album sees everyone's photos, names and comments, but the minor rule was
  only checked between the person adding and the host. A host who is friends with a 15-year-old and
  with an adult stranger to them could put both in one album (by picking both, adding one later,
  approving a request from the invite link, or adding everyone going to an event). Groups in chats
  already refuse this (`assertGroupSafe`).
- **Where:** `apps/api/src/modules/together.ts` (`whoGoesIn`, request approval), new `minorPairs` in
  `apps/api/src/lib/together.ts`.
- **Fix:** every pair in the album must pass: an adult and someone under 18 share one only as
  friends (or a teen and a guardian they accepted). People picked by name are refused
  (403 `minor_protection`); people from a chat or an event who would break it are skipped and
  counted in `skipped`; approving a request is refused.
- **Tests:** "Together albums with people under 18: keeps an adult and someone under 18 who aren't
  friends out of the same album, however they would get in" and "skips people from an event…".

### 3. Medium: live location after a friendship ends

- **What:** when someone under 18 is involved, sharing where you are needs a friendship (or family
  link). That was checked when the share started only; after unfriending, the share went on sending
  points for up to 8 hours.
- **Where:** `apps/api/src/modules/location.ts` (`POST /v1/location-shares/:id/point`).
- **Fix:** each new point checks the rule again; when it no longer holds the share stops (its point
  is deleted, everyone's card updates) and the phone gets 409 `share_ended`.
- **Test:** "sharing where you are with someone under 18: stops a live share once the friendship it
  relied on ends".

### 4. Medium: free door tickets for events that sell tickets

- **What:** every "going" answer came with an RSVP ticket, including for events whose host sells
  tickets. The door checks it in as valid, so anyone who could see the event got in without buying.
- **Where:** `apps/api/src/lib/tickets.ts` (`issueRsvpTicket`).
- **Fix:** while the event has a ticket on sale (an active ticket product for it), answering "going"
  is recorded but gives no door ticket; people get in with the tickets they buy. RSVP tickets given
  before the sale began keep working.
- **Test:** "event tickets: gives no free door ticket for answering "going" to an event that sells
  tickets".

### 5. Medium: echo videos reused outside their echo

- **What:** an echo video is stored as the maker's own media, and it holds the original creator's
  reel, which may be for followers or friends only. Posted with `echo`, it shows only to people who
  can see the original and stops showing when the original goes. But the same video could be posted
  as an ordinary public reel, added to a story, sent in a chat, added to a Together album or edited in
  the editor or Studio, which published the original to anyone and past the creator's later choices
  (blocks, deleting the reel, turning echoes off).
- **Where:** `apps/api/src/lib/recap-sharing.ts` (`assertRecapUse`, which every posting, story, chat,
  album, editor and Studio path already calls) and `apps/api/src/lib/publishing.ts`.
- **Fix:** an echo video of someone else's reel is refused everywhere (403 `echo_not_reusable`)
  except when it's posted as that echo. An echo of your own reel is not limited.
- **Test:** "echoes: only lets an echo video out as that echo, never as a plain post, a story or into
  an album".

### 6. Low: Together live updates across a block

- **What:** `together.items` ("Ada added 3") went live to every member, including people who blocked
  the person adding (they never see those items).
- **Where:** `apps/api/src/modules/together.ts` (`POST /v1/together/:id/items`).
- **Fix:** the event goes only to members with no block either way with the person adding.
- **Test:** "Together albums with people under 18: doesn't tell someone who blocked a member, live,
  that they added photos".

### 7. Low: blocked people in a seller's list of buyers

- **What:** a listing's detail for its seller lists the people who wrote about it, including people
  who blocked the seller or whom the seller blocked.
- **Where:** `apps/api/src/modules/market.ts` (`detail`).
- **Fix:** people blocked either way are left out.
- **Test:** "Market: leaves people blocked either way out of the seller's list of who wrote about a
  listing".

### 8. Low: mix updates named other chats

- **What:** `mix.updated` carried the ids of every chat the mix is shared into, to everyone in any of
  those chats.
- **Where:** `apps/api/src/lib/mixes.ts` (`publishMixChanged`).
- **Fix:** the owner hears about every chat; everyone else only about the chats they're in.
- **Test:** "mixes shared into several chats: tells each person, live, only about the chats they are
  in".

### 9. Low, not fixed: DNS rebinding window when fetching link icons

- **What:** `fetchLinkIcon` checks that the host resolves only to public addresses
  (`assertSafeWebhookUrl`), then `fetch` resolves it again. A host with a very short-lived DNS answer
  could point the second lookup at a private address. The impact is small: only
  `https://<host>/favicon.ico` is requested (a GET with no credentials), redirects are checked hop by
  hop, only up to 32 KB of a recognised image is kept, and nothing else of the response is stored or
  shown. Webhook delivery has the same window.
- **Where:** `apps/api/src/lib/link-icons.ts`, `apps/api/src/lib/webhooks.ts`.
- **What to do:** connect to the address that was checked (a custom lookup or dispatcher that pins
  it), for icons and webhooks together. It needs an HTTP agent the API doesn't have yet, so it is left
  for a change of its own.

## Checked and found sound

- **Authorization:** every route in the modules above looks the object up with the caller (own
  listing, own ticket, own share, own echo, own mix, album membership, chat membership, door team)
  and answers "not found" to anyone else. Offers can be answered only by the other side, withdrawn
  only by their maker; co-hosts can't remove the host or each other.
- **Hidden data:** a listing's place is kept snapped to about a kilometre and only whole kilometres
  are returned; approximate location shares are snapped on the server; tickets never show their
  token or code to anyone but the holder, and the export leaves both out; the door shows under-18
  guests by first name only to hosts who aren't their friend, and search matches only that; askers
  who hid their name are never returned (the inbox, answers, posts, notifications and the export all
  leave them out); album invite codes go to hosts only.
- **Tickets:** tokens are HMAC-SHA256 (96 bits kept) over the ticket id and a nonce, compared in
  constant time, canonical base64url only; transfers, refunds and RSVPs taken back change the nonce;
  check-in and transfer lock the ticket row, so a ticket can't be checked in or given away twice at
  once; failed door attempts are limited per person; production refuses to start without a
  32-character `TICKET_TOKEN_SECRET`.
- **Races:** drop stock is taken in one guarded update per product and the per-buyer limit is
  counted while that row is locked; Market's daily listing limit and one waiting offer per buyer are
  held by advisory locks; album and mix limits by row locks; the six-games and three-of-a-kind limits
  by the trigger's per-chat advisory lock (migration 0060), which counts after taking the lock.
- **SQL:** all values are parameters; the only SQL built from input is from fixed fragments (Market
  search, the guest list), with `LIKE` wildcards escaped.
- **Files and processes:** ffmpeg runs with an argument list (no shell); filter strings are built
  from numbers and enums; the credit text is escaped for Pango; temporary files live in a fresh
  `mkdtemp` folder; stored keys are made from ids and random UUIDs. Collages refuse images over 100
  megapixels before decoding and read one photo at a time; covers are capped at 4096 px; echoes are
  limited to their maximum length and the cut to 15 seconds.
- **Input:** zod schemas bound every field (coordinates, radii, prices, offsets, lengths) and drop
  unknown ones, so extra fields can't set sellers, statuses or owners.
- **CSRF:** sessions are `SameSite=Lax` cookies and bodies must be JSON, so another site can't make a
  signed-in browser change anything.
- **Realtime:** location points go only to chat members without a block either way; ticket and door
  updates only to the holder and the door team; offers and listing cards only to the two people in
  the chat, each as they see it; echo progress only to its maker.
- **Export and deletion:** the new kinds of data are in the export with other people by username;
  deletion removes or anonymises location shares, listings, offers, ratings, tickets and co-host
  places, echoes, collages, mixes, album contributions and questions.

## Accepted as designed

- Giving a ticket to a friend lets them see the event even when it's for the host's friends (the
  host allows transfers per event; `docs/product/status.md` says so).
- A game's live updates go to everyone in the chat who sees its card, as the card itself does.
- A check-in that finds a refunded, cancelled or already used ticket names its holder to the door
  team, who sold it or let them in.
